Inrank Technologies provides high-performance web engineering and organic search growth services under transparent, fixed-price packages:

Starter Launch (₹11,999)

3 Payment Terms & Invoicing

  • Upfront Milestones: Standard website projects require a 50% initiation advance deposit to begin architecture and design, with the remaining 50% due upon final staging review prior to public domain deployment.
  • Zero Hidden Fees: Quotations issued by Inrank Technologies are all-inclusive for the agreed scope. Any out-of-scope requests (e.g. additional pages, custom third-party paid API subscriptions) will be quoted separately and approved in writing.
  • Accepted Payment Modes: UPI, Bank Wire Transfer (IMPS/NEFT/RTGS), Razorpay, and Stripe for international clients. Invoices include GST where applicable.

4 100% Intellectual Property & Code Ownership Transfer

We operate under a strict Zero Vendor Lock-in philosophy.

✓ Complete Client Ownership: Upon full and final settlement of project invoices, 100% of the custom source code, HTML/CSS/JS assets, vector graphics, copy, database structures, and deployment credentials created for your project become the exclusive intellectual property of the Client.

5 Delivery Timelines & Client Cooperation

  • Starter Websites: Delivered within 5 to 7 business days from receipt of brand assets and initial deposit.
  • Business Growth Websites: Delivered within 7 to 10 business days.
  • Custom Web Applications: 2 to 3 weeks depending on API integration complexity.

Timely delivery is contingent on the Client providing necessary materials (logos, specific copy, product imagery, and domain registrar access). Delays in feedback exceeding 14 calendar days may temporarily pause development schedules.

6 365 Days Support Warranty & Revisions

All website development packages include:

  • Collaborative Revisions: Two full rounds of design and layout fine-tuning during the staging phase prior to launch.
  • 365 Days Technical Support Warranty: Includes bug fixes, browser compatibility patches, broken link rectifications, and SSL renewal assistance at zero additional charge.

7 SEO & Search Engine Disclaimers

Inrank Technologies adheres strictly to search engine quality guidelines and white-hat SEO methodologies (Core Web Vitals sub-1s optimization, Schema.org entities, high-authority citations).

While we have a proven track record of securing top-3 Google Maps and organic rankings for our clients, search algorithms (Google, Bing, Perplexity, ChatGPT Search) operate independently. No ethical agency can guarantee permanent #1 rankings, and our performance is measured against agreed transparent ranking KPIs.
