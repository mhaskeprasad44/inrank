Digital Marketing in Pune — FAQs

How much do digital marketing services cost in Pune with Inrank?

Inrank provides transparent monthly retainers starting from ₹11,999/month for Starter SEO (up to 15 keywords + Google 3-Pack), ₹18,999/month for Growth Engine (up to 30 keywords + Google Ads management), and ₹27,999/month for Enterprise Domination with Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) and Meta Ads. We do not enforce long-term lock-in contracts.

How soon can we expect measurable leads and revenue?

Paid advertising campaigns (Google Ads and Meta Ads) begin generating verified inquiries within 48 to 72 hours of launch. Organic Local SEO and Google Maps 3-Pack ranking typically exhibit significant ranking movement and call volume increases within 45 to 90 days.

Do we have to sign a long-term 6 or 12-month lock-in contract?

No. Inrank operates strictly on month-to-month retainers. We believe in earning your partnership every single month through transparent revenue attribution, verified lead generation, and continuous ROI improvements.

What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) and why is it essential?

GEO is next-generation search engine optimization engineered for AI answer engines including Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT Search, and Perplexity. Inrank structures your entity schema and authoritative citations so your brand is directly recommended when potential buyers ask AI queries like 'best B2B manufacturers in Pune' or 'top clinics in Baner'.

Do you provide digital marketing for industrial and B2B companies in PCMC and Chakan?

Yes. A significant portion of our portfolio consists of precision CNC manufacturers, engineering export firms, auto ancillary suppliers, and industrial automation brands across Bhosari MIDC, Chakan Industrial Area, Talawade, and Hinjewadi.

Which platform drives better ROI for Pune businesses: Google Ads or Meta Ads?

Google Ads captures high-intent, immediate buyers actively searching for solutions (ideal for B2B engineering, corporate services, clinics, and real estate). Meta Ads (Instagram & Facebook) excels at demand generation, visual brand storytelling, retargeting, and D2C scale. For maximum ROI, Inrank integrates both into a unified acquisition funnel.

How do you track whether leads from ads are actually turning into paying customers?

We configure end-to-end conversion tracking using Google Analytics 4 (GA4), Meta Conversions API (CAPI), and direct WhatsApp CRM event tagging. You receive a live 24/7 dashboard showing exact cost per lead, inquiry source, and revenue generated.

Can you manage our ad spend directly inside our Google and Meta ad accounts?

Yes. All ad campaigns are configured inside your own Google Ads and Meta Business Manager accounts. You retain 100% ownership of your audience data, billing, and campaign history.

What daily or monthly ad budget do we need to start running ads?

For Google Search Ads in Pune, we typically recommend an initial test ad spend of ₹15,000 to ₹30,000/month. For Meta Ads, ₹10,000 to ₹25,000/month is sufficient to test creative variations and capture qualified buyers.
