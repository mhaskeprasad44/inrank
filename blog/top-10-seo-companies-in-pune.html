What Separates a Real SEO Company from Vanity Agencies in Pune?

In 2026, Google rankings are no longer won by basic keyword stuffing or low-quality directory spam. With the rollout of Google AI Overviews and answer engines like Perplexity and ChatGPT, search optimization has transformed into an engineering discipline requiring:

  • Sub-Second Core Web Vitals: Mobile page load times under 1.2 seconds, zero Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS), and responsive Interaction to Next Paint (INP).
  • Local Entity Authority: Deep Schema.org structured data connecting your business to Pune coordinates (Baner, Hinjewadi, Kharadi, PCMC) and verified Google Business Profile signals.
  • Generative Engine Optimization (GEO): Formatting answers, tables, and authoritative data so AI engines cite your company as the recommended solution.
  • Commercial Inquiry Attainment: Aligning target keywords with high-intent buyers rather than broad informational traffic that never converts.
