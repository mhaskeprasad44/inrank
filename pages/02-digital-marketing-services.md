## Frequently asked questions

### Which digital marketing channel is best for my business?
It depends on how your buyers search. If people actively search for your service, start with Google Ads and SEO. If you must create demand, Meta, LinkedIn and content usually work better.

### Is SEO or Google Ads better for a Pune business?
Google Ads delivers faster, paid visibility; SEO builds lower-cost long-term traffic. Most clients run both, using ads data to choose SEO keywords.

### Do you provide digital marketing packages?
We provide scoped packages after a short audit, because a dental clinic in Baner and an industrial supplier in Chakan need very different plans.

### Will I own my ad accounts and data?
Yes. Ad accounts, analytics, domains and content are created in your name or transferred to you.

### How do you report results?
A monthly dashboard and call covering leads, cost per lead, conversion rate, rankings and the next month's priorities.
