## Frequently asked questions

### Is bulk WhatsApp marketing legal?
Sending to opted-in users through the official WhatsApp Business Platform is permitted. Unofficial bulk-sender tools risk number bans and we do not use them.

### What does WhatsApp API cost?
Meta charges per conversation category, plus provider and management fees. We estimate costs from your expected volume.

### Can WhatsApp connect to my CRM?
Yes, through the provider's integrations or webhooks.
