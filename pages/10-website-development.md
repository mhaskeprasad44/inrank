## Frequently asked questions

### How much does website development cost in Pune?
Costs vary with pages, design, platform and integrations. A business website, an ecommerce store and a custom web app are priced very differently; we provide a fixed-scope quote after discovery.

### How long does it take to build a website?
A standard business website typically takes three to six weeks; ecommerce and custom builds take longer depending on scope and content readiness.

### Will my website be SEO-friendly?
Yes. Technical SEO, schema markup, fast loading and clean structure are part of every build.

### Which platform should I choose?
WordPress suits content-heavy business sites, Shopify suits most ecommerce stores, and custom React/Next.js suits applications and unique functionality. We recommend based on your needs.

### Will I own the website?
Yes. Domain, hosting, code and content belong to you.

### Do you redesign existing websites?
Yes, with redirect mapping so existing rankings are protected.
