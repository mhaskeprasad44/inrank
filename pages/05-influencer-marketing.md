## Frequently asked questions

### Micro or macro influencers—which is better?
For local Pune businesses, micro creators (roughly 10k–100k followers) usually deliver better engagement and cost-efficiency than a single celebrity post.

### How do you check fake followers?
We review audience location, engagement patterns, comment quality and follower growth history before shortlisting.

### Are paid collaborations disclosed?
Yes. All paid partnerships follow ASCI influencer advertising disclosure guidelines.

### Can we reuse influencer content in ads?
Yes, if usage rights are included in the contract; we negotiate this upfront.
