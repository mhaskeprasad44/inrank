# Brand Naming Agency in Pune

Inrank creates memorable, available brand names for Pune startups and businesses through positioning workshops, creative naming territories, linguistic checks and domain and trademark pre-screening.

**[Get a Free Audit]** · **[Call / WhatsApp]**

![Brand Naming Agency in Pune – Inrank Technologies team in Pune](/images/brand-naming/brand-naming-hero.webp)

## Who this service is for

- Startups before launch
- Businesses rebranding or expanding
- New product or project launches
- Real estate projects needing project names

## What's included

- Naming brief and positioning workshop
- Naming territories and 50+ candidates
- Shortlist with rationale
- Pronunciation and meaning checks (English, Hindi, Marathi)
- Domain and social handle availability
- Trademark pre-screening (final clearance by your attorney)
- Tagline options

## Our process

1. **Brief** — Audience, values, competitors.
2. **Explore** — Generate names across territories.
3. **Screen** — Linguistic, domain and trademark checks.
4. **Present** — Shortlist with mock-ups.
5. **Finalise** — Selection and next steps for registration.
