## Frequently asked questions

### What is DLT registration?
TRAI requires businesses to register their entity, sender IDs and message templates on a DLT platform before sending commercial SMS in India.

### Promotional vs transactional SMS?
Promotional SMS carries offers and follows time and preference restrictions; transactional/service SMS carries OTPs, alerts and updates.
