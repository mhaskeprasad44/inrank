## Frequently asked questions

### How much does digital marketing cost in Pune?
Retainers depend on channels and scope. Most Pune SMEs start with one or two channels - typically SEO plus Google Ads or social - then add channels once tracking proves what works. We share a written scope and fixed monthly fee before work begins.

### How long does it take to see results?
Paid ads can generate enquiries within the first weeks once tracking and landing pages are ready. SEO usually needs three to six months for competitive Pune keywords, and longer for new domains. We do not promise guaranteed rankings.

### Do you only work with Pune businesses?
Pune is our home market and we understand local buyer behaviour, but we also work with businesses across Maharashtra and India.

### What makes Inrank different from other Pune agencies?
We build the website, run the traffic and own the tracking, so there is no blame-shifting between vendors. Every report links activity to qualified enquiries.

### Can I hire you for a single service?
Yes. Every service on this site can be taken individually.

### Do you sign long-term contracts?
We recommend a minimum period for SEO because results compound, but scopes and exit terms are agreed in writing upfront.
