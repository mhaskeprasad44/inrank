## Frequently asked questions

### How much do SEO services cost in Pune?
SEO pricing depends on site size, competition and content needs. We quote a fixed monthly retainer after the free audit, with deliverables listed line by line.

### How long does SEO take?
Low-competition and local keywords can move within weeks; competitive terms such as 'SEO company in Pune' usually take several months. Nobody can ethically guarantee a #1 ranking or 'instant' results.

### What is local SEO?
Local SEO improves visibility in Google Maps and 'near me' searches through your Google Business Profile, reviews, local citations and location-relevant content.

### Do you build backlinks?
Yes, through PR, partnerships, directories that real customers use and useful content. We do not buy link packages from private blog networks.

### Will you optimise for AI Overviews and ChatGPT?
Yes. Our SEO includes entity clarity, answer-first content and structured data that support visibility in AI-powered search.

### Can I see what you will change before you change it?
Yes. Every change is listed in a shared action log.
