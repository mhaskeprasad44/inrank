## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?
SEO targets search rankings, AEO targets direct answers and featured snippets, and GEO targets mentions and citations within generative AI responses. They share the same foundations.

### How do you measure GEO?
By tracking a fixed set of prompts for brand mentions, citations, cited URLs and AI referral traffic in analytics.

### How long does GEO take?
Initial changes can be picked up as pages are recrawled; authority-driven citations build over months.
