CRO Methodology

How Inrank optimizes websites for maximum conversion.

Open a workflow below to inspect how Inrank approaches technical diagnosis, execution standards, and measurable commercial outcomes.

01

Why do visitors land on our website and leave without inquiring?

+
Heuristic Audit & Cognitive Friction Analysis

Visitors leave when value propositions are vague, page load speeds are slow (> 2s), or primary call-to-action buttons are buried beneath decorative graphics. We audit your pages for clarity, contrast, and credibility.

The Decision Restructure above-the-fold content to present immediate, undeniable commercial value.
  1. Heatmap Diagnostic
  2. Heuristic Teardown
  3. Copywriting Restructure
02

How do you run A/B split tests without breaking our live website?

+
Client-Side & Server-Side Split Testing

We deploy controlled A/B test variations using Google Tag Manager or lightweight testing scripts, directing 50% of traffic to the new variation to measure conversion lift with statistical significance.

The Decision Only implement permanent code changes when variations prove a statistically significant win.
  1. Hypothesis Formulation
  2. Variant Deployment
  3. Significance Verification
03

How does mobile form optimization impact lead generation in India?

+
Mobile Ergonomics & 1-Tap Lead Funnels

Long 10-field forms cause 80% mobile drop-off. We replace long forms with 2-step micro-forms, auto-detect mobile keyboards (numeric for phone numbers), and add direct WhatsApp chat buttons.

The Decision Reduce form friction to the absolute minimum necessary for sales qualification.
  1. Field Pruning
  2. Input Type Optimization
  3. WhatsApp Direct Trigger

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything business owners and growth leaders need to know before initiating a sprint with Inrank Technologies.

How much traffic is required for Conversion Rate Optimization?+

For statistical significance in A/B testing, we recommend at least 3,000 to 5,000 monthly visitors, though heuristic UX audits provide immediate value at any traffic level.

How much conversion lift can we expect?+

Across our Pune client portfolio, our CRO sprints typically deliver a 35% to 80% increase in lead generation within 30 to 60 days.

Do you rewrite our website sales copy as part of CRO?+

Yes. Copywriting is often the highest-leverage conversion variable; we craft high-impact value propositions and clear benefit bullets.

Will CRO testing slow down our website?+

No. We use lightweight testing configurations that execute asynchronously without hurting Google Core Web Vitals.

What is the cost of an Inrank CRO audit sprint?+

Our standalone CRO Heuristic Audit & Action Plan is ₹14,999; full 60-day testing retainers are ₹24,999/month.
