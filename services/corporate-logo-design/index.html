What is the difference between an amateur logo and a strategic corporate mark?

+
Simplicity, Scalability & Trademark Defensibility

Amateur logos use complex 3D gradients, thin lines, and clip-art icons that blur on mobile screens and cannot be embroidered. Strategic corporate marks use clean geometric geometry with clear negative space.

The Decision Design timeless vector marks that scale effortlessly from digital favicons to physical building signage.
  1. Monochrome Contrast Test
  2. Scalability Verification
  3. Trademark Availability Screen
02

What files and formats do we receive upon final delivery?

+
Complete Vector Master Assets Suite

You receive editable vector masters (AI, EPS, SVG, PDF) alongside high-resolution raster files (transparent PNG, WebP) in full-color, reversed-white, and solid-black variations.

The Decision Provide an organized cloud folder with every format required by printers, sign makers, and web developers.
  1. Vector Export
  2. Color Profile Calibration (CMYK & RGB)
  3. Master Cloud Archive
03

Do you provide brand guidelines for fonts and colors?

+
Brand Identity Guidelines Manual

Yes. Every corporate logo project includes a 12-to-20 page Brand Guidelines manual specifying primary/secondary color palettes (Pantone, CMYK, RGB, HEX), typography hierarchy, minimum clear space, and forbidden usage.

The Decision Deliver a standardized PDF brand manual to ensure long-term visual consistency.
  1. Color Calibration
  2. Typography Pairing
  3. Guidelines Manual Assembly
