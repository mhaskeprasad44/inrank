What is the difference between a product catalog and a corporate profile brochure?

Strategic Content Architecture

A corporate profile sells your company's stability, leadership, machinery, and credibility. A product catalog provides detailed technical specifications and part numbers for procurement teams. We design both formats with disciplined visual systems.
