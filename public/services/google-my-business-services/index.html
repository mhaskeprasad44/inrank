Frequently asked questions about Google My Business services

What are Google My Business services?

Google My Business services cover the setup, verification, optimization and ongoing management of your Google Business Profile so you appear in local search and Google Maps.

Is Google My Business now called Google Business Profile?

Yes. Google renamed it Google Business Profile. People still search for "Google My Business" and "GMB", so we use both terms.

How do I rank higher in the Google map pack?

Complete every profile field, choose accurate categories, collect genuine reviews, post regularly and keep your details consistent across the web. Local links and a strong website also help.

How long does GMB optimization take to work?

Profile changes can improve visibility within a few weeks. Review growth and prominence take longer, usually two to three months.

Can you fix a suspended Google Business Profile?

Often yes. We review the cause, correct the listing against Google's guidelines and submit a reinstatement request. Success is not guaranteed in every case.

Do I need a physical shop for a Google listing?

Not always. Service-area businesses can list a service area and hide their address, if they follow Google's eligibility rules.
