Why digital PR services matter

Digital PR services earn links by giving journalists and bloggers a story. A survey, a local data point or an expert opinion can win mentions on respected sites. These links carry more weight than directory submissions. They also build brand awareness, which helps your SEO company in Pune results compound over time.

How our link building services process works

Our link building services process follows four clear stages, so you always know what is happening and why.

  • Audit: We review your site, rankings, competitors and AI visibility to find the biggest gaps.
  • Plan: We map one primary keyword and related terms to each page and set clear targets.
  • Execute: We fix technical issues, publish optimized content and earn authority signals.
  • Report: You get a monthly report covering rankings, citations, traffic and leads, with next steps.

Our standards for high authority backlinks

High authority backlinks come from sites with genuine readers and editorial control. We check each target for organic traffic, topical relevance and spam signals. We avoid private blog networks and paid link schemes that break Google's spam policies. Anchor text stays natural, with a mix of branded, partial and generic terms.

Semrush data shows how strong the Pune leaders are. Awrange ranks in the top three for many digital marketing terms largely on the strength of its domain. To compete, a newer site needs a steady flow of relevant referring domains, not a sudden spike.

How links support your other services

Backlinks lift every page they point to. We pair them with content marketing so each link lands on a page that deserves it. We also feed authority into your Google My Business services and AI visibility work, since AI tools tend to cite sources that trusted sites already reference.

Common link building mistakes we avoid

Many businesses buy bulk links, exchange links in large networks or stuff exact-match anchors. These shortcuts leave patterns that algorithms detect. We focus on relevance, editorial standards and steady pacing, so your profile looks like a brand that earns attention naturally. We keep a record of every outreach so you know where each link came from.

Reporting you can verify

Every month you receive a list of live links with source, anchor, target page and domain metrics. You can check each one yourself in Semrush or Google Search Console.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are link building services?

Link building services help you earn backlinks from other websites to yours. Search engines treat relevant, trusted links as a signal of credibility, which supports higher rankings.

Are backlinks still important for Google rankings?

Yes. Links remain a core ranking signal, though quality and relevance matter far more than volume. A few strong editorial links beat hundreds of weak ones.

What are digital PR services?

Digital PR services earn media coverage and links by pitching stories, data and expert comments to journalists and publishers. They build both backlinks and brand trust.

Do you provide dofollow backlinks?

We target editorial placements that are normally dofollow. Some trusted publishers use nofollow or sponsored tags, and we follow their policy to keep your profile safe and natural.

How many backlinks do I need to rank?

It depends on your competitors. We benchmark the referring domains of the pages you want to beat and set a monthly target based on that gap.

Can bad backlinks hurt my website?

Spammy or paid links can trigger manual actions or lose value. We audit your existing profile and advise on removal or disavow where needed.
