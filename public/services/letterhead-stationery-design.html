Mistakes to avoid with letterhead design

The most common mistakes are copying trends, using too many colours or fonts, ignoring small-size legibility and skipping brand guidelines. We avoid each of these by designing for simplicity, testing every asset at real sizes and documenting the rules so your team stays consistent.

Frequently asked questions about letterhead design

What should a company letterhead include?

A logo, company name, address, phone, email, website and legal details such as GST or CIN where needed, with clear space for the letter body.

What size is a standard letterhead in India?

The common size is A4, which is 210 by 297 mm.

Do you provide editable letterhead templates?

Yes. We provide Word and Google Docs templates along with print-ready PDFs.

Can you design a full stationery set?

Yes. We can design envelopes, folders, notepads, invoices and email signatures to match.

How long does letterhead design take?

Most letterheads take three to five working days, with a full stationery set taking one to two weeks.

Can you match my existing brand colours?

Yes. We follow your brand guidelines or build them if none exist.
