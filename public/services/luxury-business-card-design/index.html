Premium finishes explained

Foil adds a metallic accent to a logo or line. Embossing raises part of the design for a tactile feel. Spot UV adds a glossy highlight on a matte base. Thick stocks give a solid feel in the hand. We explain the cost and look of each option and prepare the layered artwork that the printer needs.

Design rules for a luxury look

Luxury design relies on restraint. We use generous white space, a limited palette, high-quality typefaces and a single standout detail. Information is limited to what the reader needs, such as name, role, phone, email and website. A QR code can link to your site or digital profile without cluttering the card. Frequently asked questions about luxury business card design

What makes a business card look luxurious?

Heavy paper, simple layouts, quality typography and a special finish such as foil or emboss create a premium feel.

What size is a standard business card in India?

A common size is 3.5 by 2 inches, or about 90 by 54 mm. We can design custom shapes where your printer supports them.

Do you print the cards?

We prepare print-ready files and can coordinate with trusted printers. Printing costs are separate.

Can I add a QR code to my card?

Yes. We place it so it scans easily without crowding the design.

How long does card design take?

Most designs take three to five working days, including revisions.

Can you design cards for a whole team?

Yes. We create a consistent template so every team member's card matches.
