3 Web Analytics & Tracking Technologies

We employ industry-standard, privacy-conscious telemetry tools to measure user experience and ensure lightning-fast page delivery:

  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4): We track aggregated traffic metrics and conversion events with IP anonymization enabled. We do not transmit personally identifiable information (PII) to Google.
  • Microsoft Clarity: We utilize Clarity to record anonymized interaction heatmaps and scroll depth to diagnose layout bottlenecks. Sensitive form inputs (passwords, payment fields) are masked by default.
  • Google Tag Manager (GTM): Facilitates tag management without executing third-party tracking without consent.
💡 We Never Sell Data: Inrank Technologies has never sold, rented, or traded personal client information to third-party data brokers or advertisers, and will never do so.

4 Client Confidentiality & NDA Protection

All commercial engagements, bespoke codebases, architectural blueprints, trade secrets, design mockups, and business strategies shared with Inrank Technologies are protected by strict Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDA).

Client code repositories, database credentials, API secrets, and server access tokens are strictly encrypted and restricted to authorized project engineers under role-based access controls.

5 Data Retention & Security Standards

Inrank Technologies enforces rigorous technical and organizational security controls:

  • End-to-End Encryption: All website traffic is protected by 256-bit TLS/SSL encryption.
  • Retention Policy: Inactive lead inquiries are securely archived or purged after 24 months, unless a commercial contract or tax regulation requires retention.
  • Access Control: Administrative access to production databases and analytics consoles is guarded by multi-factor authentication (MFA).

6 Your Legal Rights (DPDPA & GDPR)

Under applicable data protection frameworks, you have specific rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access & Summary: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: Request updates to inaccurate records or permanent deletion of your contact data.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Opt out of non-essential communications or revoke cookie consent at any time.
  • Grievance Redressal: Submit inquiries or concerns regarding data processing directly to our designated compliance desk.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at inranktech@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Privacy Request". Requests are resolved within 15 business days.
