What is Vinove Prism?+

Prism is Vinove's proprietary marketing operating intelligence system. It connects channel evidence, fully loaded cost, qualified demand, reviews, decisions and accountable execution.

How is it different from a marketing dashboard?+

A dashboard describes activity. Prism asks whether the activity created qualified demand, records the decision that follows and connects it to initiatives, tasks or recurring routines.

What does fully loaded marketing cost include?+

It can include media, people, tools, retainers and allocated shared costs. Missing cost remains visible rather than making a channel appear artificially efficient.

Is Prism available as standalone software?+

No. Prism is proprietary Vinove operating infrastructure used behind selected work and is not currently licensed or sold as standalone software.
