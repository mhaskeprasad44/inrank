Before we talk

A clear fit starts with clear expectations.

Can I buy Bond as standalone CRM software?+

No. Bond is Vinove’s proprietary operating infrastructure, not currently licensed or sold as standalone software. Contact Vinove to discuss your sales operation and whether our approach is relevant to your needs.

What does Bond bring together?+

Leads, accounts, contacts, pipeline, targets, response auditing, performance reviews and commercial proof. The emphasis is on understanding what changed and who owns the next action.

Does AI make the sales decision?+

AI fit and reply-quality assessments provide reasons for review. They support judgement; they should not be treated as guaranteed assessments of a prospect or a representative.

What should we bring to the first conversation?+

Describe where work gets stuck, how your team currently follows up, and which pipeline question is hardest to answer. A high-level description is enough to start; you do not need to send customer records.
