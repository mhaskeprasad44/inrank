The Context

About The Project & Background

HIRAGRAFIX™ (formerly Hira Printers, established 1970) is one of Mumbai's most respected commercial printing and packaging houses, operating out of Bandra Kurla Complex (BKC) and Sakinaka. They provide offset printing, luxury mono cartons, rigid boxes, wide-format indoor/outdoor signage, and custom employee welcome kits.

Inrank designed a sleek, high-trust corporate portal emphasizing tactile craftsmanship, luxury foil stamping previews, structured material specifications (GSM, textures, coatings), and an automated sample request system that positioned HIRAGRAFIX™ as the go-to partner for enterprise brand procurement.

Project Objectives

Challenges Solved

Despite 5 decades of manufacturing craftsmanship, their legacy website looked dated and did not reflect their state-of-the-art multi-color Heidelberg printing presses.

Corporate procurement teams from BKC multinationals couldn't easily browse paper stocks, GSM weights, embossing finishes, or sample kits online.

Sales staff was inundated with manual back-and-forth emails calculating basic unit price estimates for brochures and boxes.

No organic visibility for lucrative commercial terms like 'corporate gifting kits Mumbai' and 'rigid packaging manufacturer BKC'.

Frequently Asked Questions

Details about the HIRAGRAFIX™ (Hira Printers) deployment and architecture.

How did Inrank structure the product catalog for hundreds of custom print items?

We grouped products into 6 distinct core verticals with filterable tags by material type, finish, and turnaround time, making it effortless for corporate buyers to find exactly what they need in seconds.

What makes the Sample Kit request desk so effective for B2B sales?

In print, tactile touch drives conversions. By making it easy for marketing heads to receive a curated physical sample box at their office, HIRAGRAFIX converts over 65% of sample requesters into paying corporate clients.
