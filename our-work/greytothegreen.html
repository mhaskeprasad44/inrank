The Context

About The Project & Background

Grey To The Green (a division of Quantum Innovation, Bangalore) manufactures 100% organic, plant-powered household and garden wellness solutions developed under the scientific stewardship of renowned molecular biologist Dr. Anil Kush. Their signature product, Checkmite, is a breakthrough US EPA-registered neem dust-mite eliminator safe for infants and pets.

Inrank engineered a fresh, nature-inspired yet rigorously scientific digital storefront. We designed interactive 'Science Behind the Spray' modules, integrated US EPA registration badges, created clear before-and-after allergen protection infographics, and built a dedicated B2B Wholesale Portal alongside the D2C checkout funnel.

Project Objectives

Challenges Solved

Shoppers were skeptical of 'organic' claims due to greenwashing in the market, requiring rock-solid scientific proof and EPA registration credibility upfront.

The previous website presented products like academic research papers rather than benefit-driven lifestyle solutions.

Low mobile conversion rate on product detail pages with sluggish image load times.

No streamlined channel for physical organic stores, pediatric clinics, and distributors to place bulk wholesale orders.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Details about the Grey To The Green (Quantum Innovation) deployment and architecture.

How did Inrank convey scientific credibility without overwhelming shoppers?

We used progressive disclosure: simple, compelling benefit headlines (e.g., '100% Pet-Safe Dust Mite Elimination') backed by expandable 'View Molecular Science & EPA Details' drawers containing lab reports and patent citations.

How does the dual D2C + B2B wholesale workflow operate?

Consumers experience an effortless retail shopping flow, while commercial retailers and organic store chains access a prominent 'Distributor & Bulk Orders' tab with custom volume calculators and instant GST invoicing.
