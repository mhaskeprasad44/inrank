Frequently asked questions about LinkedIn marketing agency

What does a LinkedIn marketing agency do?

It manages your LinkedIn presence and campaigns, including profile optimization, content, advertising and outreach strategy, to generate B2B leads.

Is LinkedIn good for B2B lead generation?

Yes. LinkedIn lets you target by job title, industry and company size, which suits B2B sales with defined buyer roles.

How much does LinkedIn advertising cost?

Costs per click are typically higher than on other platforms, but leads can be more qualified. We set budgets based on your deal size and target cost per lead.

Should founders post on LinkedIn too?

Yes. Personal profiles often get more reach than company pages. We help founders share useful, authentic content with minimal time.

How long until LinkedIn brings leads?

Paid campaigns can produce leads within weeks. Organic authority usually takes two to three months of steady publishing.

Do you use automation tools for outreach?

We avoid aggressive automation that risks account restrictions. We recommend careful, personalised outreach within LinkedIn's rules.
