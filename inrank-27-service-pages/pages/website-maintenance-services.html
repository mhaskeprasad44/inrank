Why an SLA matters

A service level agreement puts promises in writing. It lists response times for urgent faults, the hours covered and the tasks included. You know who to contact and how quickly help arrives. This is valuable for stores and lead-generation sites, where each hour of downtime costs revenue.

Frequently asked questions about website maintenance services

What are website maintenance services?

They are ongoing tasks that keep your site updated, secure, fast and error-free, such as software updates, backups, monitoring and fixes.

How often should a website be maintained?

Security updates and backups should happen weekly or daily. Performance and SEO checks are best done monthly.

What does an SLA include?

An SLA sets covered tasks, response and resolution times, support hours and reporting, so expectations are clear.

Can you maintain a site you did not build?

Yes. We audit it first, then take it under maintenance after fixing any urgent issues.

What happens if my site is hacked?

We isolate the issue, restore a clean backup, remove malware, patch the cause and report what happened.

Does maintenance include content updates?

Small edits are included in most plans. Larger changes use monthly improvement hours or a separate quote.
