Why Conversions API matters

Browser restrictions and ad blockers hide many conversions from the Meta pixel. The Conversions API sends events from your server, which recovers signal and improves optimization. We implement it with deduplication, so events are not double counted. Better signal means the algorithm finds buyers more precisely, which lowers cost per result over time.

Frequently asked questions about Meta ads agency

What does a Meta ads agency do?

A Meta ads agency plans and manages paid campaigns across Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and Audience Network to generate leads, sales or awareness.

Are Instagram ads worth it for small businesses?

Yes, when targeting and creative fit your audience. Local and niche businesses can reach buyers cheaply with the right offer and a clear call to action.

What is Meta Conversions API?

It is a tool that sends conversion events directly from your server to Meta, improving tracking accuracy where the browser pixel loses data.

How much should I budget for Meta ads?

Start with enough budget to gather at least a few dozen results a week per ad set. We set exact numbers once we know your cost per lead goals.

How often should ad creative change?

Watch frequency and click-through rate. Many accounts refresh creative every two to four weeks, but the data should decide.

Can you run Facebook and Instagram ads together?

Yes. Meta places ads across both, and we can adjust placements when one performs better.
