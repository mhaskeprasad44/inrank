PPC management step by step

We begin with goals and a cost per lead target. We then build tightly themed ad groups, write several ad variants and launch with conversion tracking in place. In the first weeks we remove poor search terms and shift budget to the best segments. After that we test new offers and pages. Our Google Ads agency and Meta ads agency teams handle platform specifics.

How our PPC services process works

Our PPC services process follows four clear stages, so you always know what is happening and why.

  • Goals: We agree on target cost per lead, audiences and the conversion events that matter.
  • Build: We set up campaigns, creative and tracking so every action is measured.
  • Optimize: We review search terms, audiences and creative weekly and move budget to what works.
  • Scale: We grow spend only where return is proven, and report in plain language each month.

Lowering cost per lead

Cost per lead falls when relevance, landing page and offer all improve. We raise quality scores through closer keyword-to-ad matching and send visitors to focused landing pages. We also use conversion rate optimization to turn more of the same traffic into enquiries. Google's Ads Help Centre is our reference for policy and feature updates.

PPC alongside SEO

PPC fills the gap while SEO builds. We use paid search data to learn which phrases convert, then bring them into our SEO programme. Over time, strong organic rankings can lower your dependence on paid clicks.

What we need from you

To start, we need access to your ad accounts or permission to create new ones in your name, your conversion goals, your average deal or order value and any brand assets. If you have past campaign data, share it so we can keep what worked and drop what did not.

Mistakes to avoid with PPC services

The most common mistakes are launching without conversion tracking, sending all traffic to a homepage, changing too many settings at once and judging results too early. We avoid each of these by setting measurement first, using focused pages and making one controlled change at a time.

Frequently asked questions about PPC services

What are PPC services?

PPC services plan and manage paid ads where you pay only when someone clicks. They include research, ad creation, bidding, tracking and optimization.

Which PPC platform is best?

It depends on your buyers. Google Ads suits high-intent search, Meta suits visual and consumer products, and LinkedIn suits B2B decision makers.

How much should I spend on PPC?

Choose a budget that lets campaigns gather meaningful data, then scale based on cost per lead. We suggest a start after keyword research.

How fast can PPC bring leads?

Leads can arrive within days. Efficient performance typically needs a few weeks of testing.

How do I know PPC is working?

We track conversions, cost per lead and return on ad spend, and connect them to actual sales where possible.

Do you charge a percentage of ad spend?

We quote a clear management fee based on scope. There is no hidden mark-up on media spend.
