Shopify SEO that earns organic sales

Shopify gives a strong base, but it needs careful tuning. We optimize collection and product titles, write unique descriptions, add Product and Review schema and manage duplicate URLs created by collections. How our Shopify store development process works

Our Shopify store development process follows four clear stages, so you always know what is happening and why.

  • Discover: We learn your audience, offers, competitors and the keywords the pages must win.
  • Design: We plan structure and layouts, then share clickable designs for your feedback.
  • Build: We develop fast, secure, mobile-first pages with schema and tracking built in.
  • Launch: We test, go live, monitor and train your team, with support available afterwards.

Conversion-focused store design

Online shoppers decide quickly. We design product pages with clear images, price, delivery details and reviews near the buy button. We simplify checkout and add cart recovery. For paid traffic we create focused landing pages and connect your store to Meta ads and Google Ads.

Mistakes to avoid with Shopify store development

The most common mistakes are choosing a platform before defining needs, ignoring page speed, hiding content behind scripts and skipping redirects when moving pages. We avoid each of these by planning structure first, testing on real phones and protecting your existing search rankings during launch.

Frequently asked questions about Shopify store development

What does Shopify store development include?

It includes store setup, theme customization, product and collection structure, payments, shipping, app integration and optimization for speed and SEO.

How long does it take to build a Shopify store?

A standard store can launch in two to four weeks. Custom features and large catalogues take longer.

How much does a Shopify store cost?

Costs include the Shopify plan, theme, apps and our development fee. We quote after reviewing your catalogue and features.

Is Shopify good for SEO?

Yes. It offers clean URLs, sitemaps and fast hosting, and we add product schema and content to improve rankings.

Can you migrate my store to Shopify?

Yes. We migrate products, customers and key URLs, and set redirects to protect traffic.

Do you set up Indian payment gateways?

Yes. We configure gateways and methods popular in India, subject to Shopify availability and your account approval.
