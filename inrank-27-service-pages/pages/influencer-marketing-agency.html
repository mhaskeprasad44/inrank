How our influencer marketing agency process works

Our influencer marketing agency process follows four clear stages, so you always know what is happening and why.

  • Goals: We agree on target cost per lead, audiences and the conversion events that matter.
  • Build: We set up campaigns, creative and tracking so every action is measured.
  • Optimize: We review search terms, audiences and creative weekly and move budget to what works.
  • Scale: We grow spend only where return is proven, and report in plain language each month.

Compliance and disclosure

Indian advertising rules require clear disclosure of paid partnerships. We follow the ASCI influencer guidelines and make sure every post carries proper labels. This protects your brand and the creator from complaints.

Turning creator content into assets

Great creator content can be reused. With permission, we turn it into ads through our Meta ads agency team, add it to landing pages and share it on your channels through social media marketing. We also feature creator reviews on your site, which supports SEO with fresh, trusted content.

Measuring return

We report reach, engagement, clicks, conversions and cost per result, so you can compare creators and decide where to invest next.

Mistakes to avoid with influencer marketing agency

The most common mistakes are launching without conversion tracking, sending all traffic to a homepage, changing too many settings at once and judging results too early. We avoid each of these by setting measurement first, using focused pages and making one controlled change at a time.

Frequently asked questions about influencer marketing agency

What does an influencer marketing agency do?

It finds suitable creators, manages negotiation and briefing, oversees content and measures campaign results for your brand.

How much does influencer marketing cost?

Fees depend on follower size, engagement, platform and content type. Micro creators cost far less than celebrities, and we match budget to goals.

Are micro influencers better than big influencers?

Often they give better engagement and value, especially for niche or local audiences. The best mix depends on your objective.

How do you track influencer ROI?

We use unique links, coupon codes, UTM tags and platform analytics to connect creator posts to clicks and sales.

Do influencers need to disclose paid posts?

Yes. Indian regulations expect clear labels such as "ad" or "paid partnership". We enforce this in every campaign.

Can influencer marketing help local Pune businesses?

Yes. Local creators can drive footfall and enquiries for restaurants, salons, coaching centres, real estate and events.
