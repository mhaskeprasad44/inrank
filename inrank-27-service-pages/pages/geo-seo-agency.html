What generative engine optimization means

Generative engine optimization focuses on being included in a synthesised answer, not only on ranking as a link. Generative tools retrieve passages from the web, weigh their trustworthiness and compose a reply with citations. Pages that state facts clearly, name their sources and sit on trusted domains tend to be retrieved more often. Semrush India data puts "generative engine optimization" at about 2,400 searches a month and "GEO SEO" at 1,600.

Frequently asked questions about GEO SEO agency

What is a GEO SEO agency?

A GEO SEO agency optimizes your content and brand so that generative AI engines like Perplexity, ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews can find, trust and cite you in their answers.

What does GEO stand for in SEO?

GEO stands for generative engine optimization. It is the practice of improving how often and how favourably AI-generated answers mention your brand.

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO aims to rank pages in a results list. GEO aims to be cited inside AI-written answers, which depends on clear passages, trusted sources and consistent brand facts.

Can you get my brand cited in Perplexity?

We improve the signals that influence citation, such as clarity, freshness and third-party mentions. Results vary by query, so we do not promise specific placements.

How do you measure GEO results?

We run a fixed set of prompts each month and track brand mentions, citation links, share of voice against competitors and referral traffic from AI platforms.

Is GEO useful for local businesses?

Yes. Many people ask AI tools for local recommendations. Consistent business data, reviews and local mentions help your business appear in those answers.
