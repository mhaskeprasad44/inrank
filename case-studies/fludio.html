The Context

About The Project & Background

Fludio Water Solutions is a premier Pune-based engineering firm specializing in turnkey architectural water features, computer-choreographed musical fountains, interactive dry-deck plazas, and resort lagoon displays for township builders, luxury hotels, and civic municipal projects across India.

Inrank engineered a fluid, ultra-luxurious visual portal utilizing progressive lazy-loaded video showcases, interactive fountain spray height selector cards, and an architectural RFP consultation funnel that positions Fludio as India's foremost aquatic engineering authority.

Project Objectives

Challenges Solved

Previous web footprint failed to communicate the breathtaking visual majesty and precision PLC automation of their aquatic installations.

Architects and landscape planners couldn't easily download engineering CAD specifications and nozzle spray pattern schematics.

Struggled to attract high-ticket B2B commercial real estate builders directly, relying on word-of-mouth intermediaries.

Heavy unoptimized video files were freezing mobile browsers on 4G networks.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Details about the Fludio Water Solutions deployment and architecture.

How did Inrank keep the website fast despite heavy video content?

We implemented adaptive progressive video loading using Intersection Observers, lightweight WebM encoding, and responsive poster frames so videos only buffer when visible on screen.

What makes Fludio's RFP consultation form convert so well?

Instead of generic contact boxes, the form speaks the architect's language - asking for project type (Township, Resort, Plaza), water basin dimensions, and choreographic requirements.
